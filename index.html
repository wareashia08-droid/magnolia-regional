Before you arrive

Visiting hours

General visiting runs 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM every day, with up to two visitors at a bedside. Some units, such as intensive care, set their own hours, so check with the care team first.

What to bring

Bring a photo ID, your insurance card, your appointment details and a current list of medications. For an overnight stay, add comfortable clothes, toiletries and phone chargers. Leave jewelry and valuables at home.

Patient forms

Registration and consent forms are available at the front desk. Arrive 15 minutes early for a first visit so there is time to complete them.

Accessibility

All public entrances are step-free, and wheelchairs are available at the main entrance. Interpreters, including sign language, can be arranged at no cost. Mention it when you request an appointment.

Family support

Family waiting areas are on every floor. Chaplains and social workers can help with emotional support, care planning and getting home safely.

Insurance and billing

Common questions

Do I need a referral to book an appointment?

You can book primary care without a referral. Most specialty visits and scans need a referral or order from your clinician, and some insurance plans require one too.

What insurance do you accept?

We accept Medicare, Georgia Medicaid and most major employer and Marketplace plans. See Insurance and billing, or call (555) 014-7822 to confirm your plan.

Can children visit patients?

Yes. Children under 12 can visit with an adult during visiting hours. Some units, such as intensive care, may limit young visitors.

How do I get my test results?

Your care team shares results at a follow-up visit or by phone. Once the patient portal launches, results will appear there too.

Do you offer interpreters?

Yes. Free interpreters, including American Sign Language, are available for every visit. Mention it when you request an appointment.

Where should I go in an emergency?

Call 911 for anything life-threatening, such as chest pain, trouble breathing or sudden signs of stroke. Our emergency department is open 24 hours a day at the east entrance off Magnolia Grove Way. Please do not use the request form for urgent needs.

Should I go to the emergency department or call my doctor?

Go to the emergency department for serious or sudden problems, like a bad injury, severe pain or a high fever in a baby. For colds, minor rashes or medication refills, call your primary care team first.
