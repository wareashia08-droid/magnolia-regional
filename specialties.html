Heart and vascular

Cardiologists and heart nurses diagnose and treat conditions of the heart and blood vessels, using tests such as EKGs, echocardiograms and stress tests.

Common reasons people visit

  • High blood pressure
  • Chest pain or a racing heartbeat
  • High cholesterol
  • Follow-up care after a heart attack

Getting started: Most visits start with a referral from your primary care team.

Neurology

Neurologists care for the brain, spine and nerves, from long-term conditions to recovery after a stroke.

Common reasons people visit

  • Stroke prevention and recovery
  • Migraines and frequent headaches
  • Seizures and epilepsy
  • Memory changes

Getting started: Referral recommended. If someone shows sudden signs of stroke, call 911 right away.

Cancer care

One cancer team handles screening, diagnosis, treatment and support, with a nurse navigator to guide you from the first appointment onward.

Common reasons people visit

  • Cancer screening
  • Chemotherapy and infusion
  • Help understanding a new diagnosis
  • Support groups for patients and families

Getting started: Ask your primary care team for a referral, or send us a request and a nurse navigator will call you.

Surgery

Surgeons perform planned and same-day procedures, and the care team helps you prepare beforehand and recover afterward.

Common reasons people visit

  • Hip and knee replacement
  • Hernia repair
  • Gallbladder surgery
  • Same-day outpatient procedures

Getting started: Starts with a referral and a pre-surgery visit.

Women's health and maternity

Care before, during and after pregnancy, plus routine women's health visits throughout life.

Common reasons people visit

  • Prenatal check-ups and ultrasounds
  • Labor and delivery
  • Recovery and feeding support after birth
  • Annual well-woman visits

Getting started: No referral needed for most visits. Birth center tours are available for expecting families.

Request an appointment Meet Dr. Sofia Marín

Pediatrics

Pediatricians care for babies, children and teens, and help families with growth and development questions.

Common reasons people visit

  • Well-child check-ups
  • Vaccines
  • Sick visits
  • School and sports physicals

Getting started: No referral needed.

Request an appointment Meet Dr. Rosa Delgado

Rehabilitation

Physical, occupational and speech therapists build a plan around your goals, whether that is walking after surgery or getting back to work.

Common reasons people visit

  • Recovery after surgery or injury
  • Rehab after a stroke
  • Balance and mobility problems
  • Speech and swallowing therapy

Getting started: Usually starts with an order from your doctor or surgeon.

Request an appointment Meet Dr. Mateo Silva

Lab services

Our lab processes blood tests and other samples, and your care team shares the results with you.

Common reasons people visit

  • Blood tests
  • Urine tests
  • Tests your clinician orders before a visit
  • Fasting blood work

Getting started: Most tests need an order from your clinician. Some, like fasting blood work, need a little preparation.
